    About ControlRoles

    The job board for project controls professionals, built by a practising planning manager.

    Why it exists

    Planning, cost, contracts and claims roles are hard to find on general job boards. They are mixed in with thousands of unrelated jobs, the same advert appears several times through different agencies, and you often can't tell whether you're even allowed to apply. ControlRoles lists only project controls roles in construction, infrastructure and energy, taken straight from the employers, with the facts that decide whether a role is worth your time.

    What you'll find here

    • Roles from the source. Every listing comes from the employer's own careers site, and you apply there. We are not a recruitment agency and never see your application.
    • Seven disciplines. Planning, project controls, cost and commercial, contracts, claims, risk and project management, from mid-level to director.
    • "Can I apply?" labels. We flag postings that are for nationals or citizens only, need security clearance or don't offer visa sponsorship.
    • The requirements at a glance. Years of experience, degree, certificates and software, summarised from each posting.
    • Expected salary and benefits. Clearly marked estimates for each role, employer and country, so you can compare offers.

    How it works

    Twice a day we check the careers sites of the developers, consultancies and contractors on the board and keep the project controls roles. Roles that close drop off at the next check, and postings older than 90 days are removed. Each job page shows when its link was last checked.

    Free and Pro

    Every job, every filter, the eligibility labels and job alerts are free. Pro adds expected pay for every role, daily alerts for only the roles you can apply for, employer follows and a monthly market report. During our launch, Pro is free for a limited time.

    Privacy policy

    Last updated 4 November 2026

    This policy explains what information ControlRoles (controlroles.com) collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: you can use the whole site without telling us who you are, we only use analytics cookies if you accept them, we don't use advertising cookies, and we never see your job applications.

    1. Using the site without an account

    You don't need an account to search jobs. If you click Accept on the cookie notice, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and jobs are viewed, so we can improve the site. It sets cookies and receives your IP address (shortened) and basic device and browser details. If you click Decline, no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. We don't use advertising cookies and we don't sell data about visitors.

    2. Information kept only in your browser

    Some features remember your choices on your own device, using your browser's local storage: saved roles and their status, your filters, Insights choices, followed employers, CV text you paste into the CV check, and whether you closed the launch banner. This information stays in your browser and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for controlroles.com.

    3. Job alerts

    If you sign up for job alerts, we collect your email address and your alert choices (disciplines, region, country, seniority, and whether you want Pro alerts). We use them only to send you the alerts you asked for and, during the launch offer, to tell you before Pro stops being free. We send a confirmation email first, and the alert only starts after you click the link in it. Every alert email has an unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing hello@controlroles.com.

    4. Applying for jobs

    When you click Apply, you leave ControlRoles and go to the employer's own careers site. We don't receive your CV, application or any information you give the employer. The employer's privacy policy applies there.

    5. Information about job listings

    Listings are copied from employers' public careers sites. They contain information about the role and the employer, not about individual people.

    6. Services we rely on

    • Hosting: the site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and keep them secure.
    • Fonts: the site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded.
    • Analytics: Google Analytics (Google), only if you accept analytics cookies.
    • Alerts: if you sign up, Cloudflare (which stores your alert details) and Resend (which sends the alert emails), both acting on our behalf.

    We don't sell or rent personal information to anyone.

    7. How long we keep it

    We keep your alert details while your alert is active and delete them within 30 days of you unsubscribing or asking us to delete them.

    8. Your rights

    You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent to receiving alerts at any time. Email hello@controlroles.com. We handle personal data in line with applicable data protection laws, including the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

    9. Changes

    If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page, and tell alert subscribers by email about any important change.

    Questions

    Where do the jobs come from?

    From each employer's own careers site, checked twice a day. You apply there, so your application goes straight to the employer.

    What does "Can I apply?" mean?

    We read each posting for limits such as nationals only, citizens only, security clearance or no visa sponsorship, and label the role, so you don't spend time on jobs you can't get.

    How are the salary estimates made?

    From multiple data sources for each country, adjusted for the employer, level and country. They are estimates, clearly marked, and never an offer.

    Is Pro really free?

    Yes, for a limited time during our launch, with no card needed. When card payments open we'll email you first; you only pay if you choose to continue, and launch members keep a founding-member price.
